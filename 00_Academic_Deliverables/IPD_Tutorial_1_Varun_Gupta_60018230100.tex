\section*{PART B --- Project Title, Background \& Problem Statement}

\subsection*{1. Project Title}
\textbf{FinReg-SLM: A Version-Aware, Multi-Agency Retrieval-Augmented Fine-Tuned Small Language Model with Native Chain-of-Thought Reasoning and Direct Preference Optimization for Indian Financial Regulatory Compliance}

\subsection*{2. Comprehensive Background \& Motivation}
\textbf{Regulatory Environment in India:} Indian financial institutions operate within an exceptionally complex, multi-jurisdictional legal ecosystem. Thirteen statutory regulatory authorities---including the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), and the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND)---regularly publish Master Directions, circulars, notifications, and compliance guidelines. Alongside 25 governing Parliamentary Acts (e.g., RBI Act 1934, Companies Act 2013, SEBI Act 1992, DPDP Act 2023), over 18,242 active statutory documents govern scheduled commercial banks, NBFCs, and fintechs. Non-compliance triggers severe financial penalties, compounding interest charges, and statutory license revocations.

\textbf{Temporal Volatility \& Superseded Clauses:} Compliance circulars are continuously amended, clarified, or partially superseded. For instance, a 2025 SEBI amendment to the Takeover Regulations modified share valuation guidelines while granting a 9-month transitional window. Standard semantic vector search fails in this setting: older 2018 circulars often match query keywords closely and are presented as active law, causing temporal hallucinations.

\textbf{Data Privacy \& the DPDP Act 2023:} Deploying cloud-hosted proprietary Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4o or Gemini 2.5 introduces critical legal liability. Uploading internal financial audit trails, banking logs, and confidential client communications to third-party offshore APIs violates data localization mandates under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. Financial institutions require self-hosted, on-premises language models.

\textbf{Epistemic Limits \& Hallucinations:} Base language models suffer from three acute failure modes in statutory QA: (1) \emph{Hallucination of Authority}, inventing plausible but fictitious circular numbers; (2) \emph{Distractor Sensitivity}, where surrounding non-relevant text confuses the model; and (3) \emph{Sycophancy}, where models answer queries based on false user premises instead of rejecting them.

\textbf{The FinReg-SLM Approach:} FinReg-SLM adapts a 7B Small Language Model (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B) for on-premises execution inside 4.5 GB VRAM on consumer hardware (8GB GPU). By integrating a version-aware hybrid retrieval engine (Dense BGE-small + BM25 + Reciprocal Rank Fusion $k=60$ + temporal date filtering) with a Two-Stage Alignment strategy (Stage 1 QLoRA SFT on Chain-of-Thought traces + Stage 2 Native DPO on epistemic preference pairs), FinReg-SLM delivers 100\% verifiable citation grounding and calibrated refusal.

\subsection*{3. Standardized Problem Statement}
Indian financial institutions operating under 13 statutory bodies lack automated, version-aware compliance verification tools. Existing general-purpose LLMs exhibit a 17\%--33\% hallucination rate, fail to track statutory circular amendments, confabulate answers on out-of-scope inquiries, and violate data sovereignty mandates under the DPDP Act 2023. This project designs, implements, and benchmarks an on-premises Small Language Model architecture providing verifiable citation grounding, calibrated epistemic abstention, and version-aware multi-agency reasoning on consumer-grade hardware.

\section*{PART E --- Literature Synthesis}
\textbf{SLMs vs. Cloud Models:} Domain-adapted Small Language Models (3B--8B) using Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (QLoRA)~\cite{biderman2024lora} and Direct Preference Optimization~\cite{rafailov2023dpo} achieve specialized statutory accuracy while maintaining on-premises data privacy.

\textbf{Hybrid Retrieval \& Temporal Validity:} Lexical BM25 and dense semantic embeddings fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion ($k=60$) coupled with explicit date filtering (\texttt{effective\_date}) eliminate temporal obsolescence in dynamic circulars~\cite{kim2026jurisdiction}.

\textbf{Epistemic Abstention \& Sycophancy:} Training models exclusively on positive QA pairs induces severe sycophancy~\cite{magesh2025hallucination}. Incorporating negative preference pairs forces the model to actively refuse when statutory authority is absent.

\textbf{Data Sovereignty under DPDP Act 2023:} Deploying 4-bit quantized 7B models on local hardware eliminates third-party cloud data leakage, meeting strict regulatory compliance mandates.

\section*{PART F --- Research Gaps, Applied Fixes \& Empirical Gains}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Gap 1 (RAG vs FT Dichotomy~\cite{ovadia2024knowledge}):} Naive RAG is confused by legal distractors. \emph{Fix:} Two-Stage RAFT with \texttt{<think>} traces. \emph{Gain:} Core statutory compliance rose to 94.43\%; IndiaFinBench Regulatory Interpretation scored 90.29\%.
    \item \textbf{Gap 2 (Hallucination of Authority~\cite{magesh2025hallucination}):} Fictitious circular citations. \emph{Fix:} Verbatim citation verification + DPO margin loss. \emph{Gain:} Citation grounding reached 99.17\%; phantom citations dropped to 0.0\%.
    \item \textbf{Gap 3 (Temporal Amnesia~\cite{kim2026jurisdiction}):} Retrieving superseded circulars. \emph{Fix:} Date metadata filtering + RRF ($k=60$) + temporal DPO. \emph{Gain:} Contradiction Detection scored 79.00\%; Temporal Reasoning scored 75.78\%.
    \item \textbf{Gap 4 (Epistemic Inability to Abstain~\cite{magesh2025hallucination}):} Answering ungrounded questions. \emph{Fix:} Negative preference DPO alignment. \emph{Gain:} Refusal quality rose from 0.0\% to 82.50\%; premise rejection to 58.00\%.
    \item \textbf{Gap 5 (Catastrophic Forgetting~\cite{biderman2024lora}):} Degrading instruction formatting. \emph{Fix:} All-linear QLoRA ($r=64, \alpha=128$) in 4-bit NF4. \emph{Gain:} 100.0\% structural ChatML protocol conformance.
    \item \textbf{Gap 6 (Non-Western Regulatory Evaluation Void~\cite{pall2026indiafinbench}):} Benchmarks ignore Indian law. \emph{Fix:} Evaluated on internal G7 (300 Qs) and external IndiaFinBench (324 Qs). \emph{Gain:} 80.98\% macro accuracy, outperforming open base 7B/8B models.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{PART G \& H --- Research Question \& Objectives}
\textbf{Research Question:} \emph{How does combining a version-aware multi-agency hybrid retrieval pipeline with a Two-Stage Aligned Reasoning Model (Stage 1 QLoRA SFT on Chain-of-Thought traces + Stage 2 Native DPO on epistemic preference pairs using DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B) compare against baseline small language models in achieving factual accuracy, 100\% citation grounding, and calibrated epistemic abstention across 13 Indian financial regulatory authorities under on-premises DPDP Act 2023 constraints?}

\textbf{Core Objectives:} (1) Index 18,242 verified documents across 13 authorities in SQLite WAL mode; (2) Implement hybrid Dense + BM25 retrieval with RRF ($k=60$) and temporal filtering; (3) Synthesize 257 golden CoT traces and 257 DPO preference pairs; (4) Train DeepSeek-R1-7B on an 8GB consumer GPU; (5) Export 15.2 GB standalone FP16 weights for private deployment; (6) Benchmark on 300 G7 queries and 324 IndiaFinBench questions via a 70B LLM Judge.

\subsection*{15. Future Scope \& Institutional Scaling Roadmap}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Scaling to 14B/32B/70B Open Models:} Fine-tuning DeepSeek-R1-Distill-14B/32B or LLaMA-3.3-70B on institutional multi-GPU compute (e.g., A100/H100) will expand reasoning depth, projected to achieve $>$91.5\% accuracy and surpass proprietary cloud models on-premises.
    \item \textbf{Program-Aided Compliance (Python REPL Sandbox):} Integrating an automated Python execution sandbox inside \texttt{<think>} traces to execute multi-step compound calculations, pushing numerical reasoning from 69.18\% to 98\%+.
    \item \textbf{Online Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO):} Transitioning to online GRPO with rule-based reward functions for statutory circular citations and mathematical verification.
    \item \textbf{Autonomous 24/7 Regulatory Monitoring Daemon:} Deploying background daemons to monitor daily gazettes, updating the SQLite WAL index with real-time SSE compliance alerts.
\end{enumerate}
